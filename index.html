Sixteen wooden pieces, each with four traits: tall or short, light or dark, square or round, hollow or solid. Line up four pieces that share a trait, in a row, a column or a diagonal, and you win.

The twist: you never choose the piece you place. Your opponent picks it for you, and you pick one for them.

Free, and nothing else

No ads, no tracking, no account, nothing to buy. Play against the computer on three levels, or against a friend on the same device, in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Chinese or Japanese.
