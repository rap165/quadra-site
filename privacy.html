Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Quadra Board collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

What the app stores

Your settings (such as the difficulty, the table and whether sound is on) and an unfinished game are saved on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting of its own.
  • No connection to the internet during play.

Motion sensors

On iPhone and iPad, the app reads the motion sensors only while the pieces lie on the table after a new game, so you can tilt and shake them. These readings are used on the spot and are not stored or sent.

Apple

If you download the app or test it through TestFlight, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share crash reports or feedback with developers in your device's settings or in TestFlight, Apple passes on only what you agreed to share.
