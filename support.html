How to play

Each of the 16 pieces has four traits: tall or short, light or dark, square or round, hollow or solid. Line up four pieces that share a trait, in a row, a column or a diagonal. You don't choose the piece you place: your opponent hands it to you, and you hand one to them. In the Advanced rules, a 2×2 square counts too. The rules are also in the app, under Settings › How to Play.

Tips

  • Tap a piece to hand it over and tap a hole to place it, or pick pieces up and drag them.
  • The light bulb gives a hint. Long-press it to sweep the pieces off the board.
  • Learn Mode (in Settings) shows which moves win, lose or draw.
  • After a new game, tilt or shake your iPhone to move the pieces about; tap to put them back.
